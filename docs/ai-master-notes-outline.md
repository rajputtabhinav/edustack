# AI Master Notes Outline

## Product Positioning
- Format: exam-style AI notes PDF
- Target length: 150-200 pages
- Audience: students, beginners, self-learners, and revision-focused users
- Style: structured notes, short explanations, bullet summaries, examples, probable questions, and MCQs

## Chapter Plan
1. Introduction to AI and Modern AI Systems
2. Machine Learning Basics
3. Deep Learning Foundations
4. Natural Language Processing and Large Language Models
5. Prompt Engineering and Prompting Patterns
6. AI Tools and Practical Workflows
7. AI for Students, Creators, Business, and Coding
8. Ethics, Safety, Bias, and Limitations
9. AI Careers, Use Cases, and Future Trends
10. Revision Zone: summaries, key terms, probable questions, MCQs, and short-answer practice

## Chapter Format
Each chapter should include:
- chapter intro
- concept summaries
- bullet notes for quick revision
- real examples and use cases
- probable exam/viva questions
- MCQs with answer key
- short-answer revision prompts

## PDF Production Workflow
1. Generate chapter source content in smaller batches.
2. Review and normalize terminology, tone, and difficulty.
3. Add revision summaries and MCQs chapter by chapter.
4. Typeset into a polished PDF layout.
5. Upload the final PDF to durable object storage.
6. Save the final file URL and metadata into the EduStack product record.
